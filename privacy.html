Privacy

Applies to Thermocline for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch. Last updated 16 November 2026.

In short: Thermocline doesn't collect your data. There's no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the developer runs no servers. Your logbook stays on your devices and, if you turn on sync, in your own iCloud.

Where your logbook is kept

Your dives, sites, gear, buddies, cards and photos are stored on your device. If you turn on Sync with iCloud, they're also stored in the private iCloud database of your own Apple Account, so your other devices can see them. Apple holds that data under Apple's privacy policy, and the developer can't read it.

You can export your whole logbook at any time (UDDF, CSV or MacDive XML), and deleting the app removes the copy on that device. To remove the iCloud copy, turn off sync and delete the app's data in your device's iCloud settings.

Permissions the app asks for

Each one is asked only when you use the feature, and you can say no and keep using the rest of the app.

What these features read stays in your logbook on your devices and in your iCloud. It isn't sent to the developer.

Services from Apple

Maps, place search and place names come from Apple Maps. When you view a map, search for a place or name a position, Apple receives that request under Apple's privacy policy. The 73,000+ built-in dive sites and wrecks are searched on your device, with no network connection.

Links you add

If you add a YouTube or Vimeo link to a dive, Thermocline stores the link. It opens in your browser only when you tap it.

The beta

The beta is delivered through Apple's TestFlight. Apple may share with the developer the crash reports and feedback you choose to send, and basic details about your device and the build, as described in Apple's TestFlight terms. That information is used only to fix and improve Thermocline.

This website

thermoclineapp.com uses no cookies, analytics or tracking. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub and Cloudflare, which serve the site, may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security.

Children

Thermocline isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect anyone's personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Changes won't reduce your control over your own logbook.
