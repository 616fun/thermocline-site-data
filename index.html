Features

Everything your dives recorded, in one logbook

Built by a tech and cave diver for recreational, technical and cave divers who want their numbers straight.

Download by Bluetooth or cable

Straight from your dive computer to your iPhone or Mac, adding the site, buddies, gear and conditions as each dive comes in. The Mac also downloads by USB and serial cable.

Bring your whole history

Import from MacDive, a Shearwater Cloud database, UDDF or CSV, and choose which dives to add. Anything it can't read is listed for you, never silently dropped.

Safety values first

Six key values sit in a fixed strip at the top of every dive. Show tank, SAC, temperature, ppO₂, CNS and ascent rate as strips or one overlay.

SAC that shows its work

Worked out from your tank's water capacity with real-gas arithmetic, so it's right at any fill. Tap How? to see every input. Sidemount, doubles and multi-gas dives are counted properly.

73,000+ sites and wrecks worldwide

Find and name a dive site anywhere in the world, even without signal, from open site lists built into the app. Your own sites always come first.

Apple Watch at the surface

Mark the site from your wrist when you surface. The position waits in your logbook for you to name it later.

Two computers, one dive

Dive with a backup computer? Thermocline offers to merge the two recordings, or join a dive that was split in two. It never does either without asking.

Synced through your iCloud

Your logbook, gear, buddies and photos follow you between iPhone, iPad and Mac. Restore points can take you back to before an import or merge.

Always a way out

Export everything, any time, as UDDF, CSV or MacDive XML. Your logbook is never locked in.

Also in the app

Gear and tanks, including + ratings Buddies, linked to Contacts Certification cards Dive photos and video links Search by site, buddy, gas, notes, date or #number Summary of depths, gases and records Metric or imperial 17 languages

How it's built

A logbook you can trust with your numbers

Three rules shape every screen.

01

A logbook, not a planner

Thermocline shows what your dive computer recorded. It never calculates decompression, and it never puts its own guess in place of your computer's numbers.

02

Your data stays yours

No account, no ads, no analytics, and no developer servers. Your logbook lives on your devices and in your own iCloud, and exports in full whenever you like. Privacy policy

03

Warn, never block

If a download or a sync only partly works, you're told what happened and nothing is thrown away. Edits keep the computer's original beside your correction.

Questions

Before you join

Already testing? The Guide and Troubleshooting pages cover the app step by step.

Does it work on Android, Windows or the web?

No. Thermocline is made only for Apple devices: iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch. It's built with Apple's own tools so it can work closely with iCloud, Bluetooth, Photos and Maps.

Which dive computers does it work with?

Bluetooth downloads are tested with Shearwater Perdix and Perdix 2, on iPhone, iPad and Mac. The Mac also downloads by USB and serial cable from many other computers, including Suunto, older Oceanic, Aeris and Sherwood, Uwatec and Scubapro, Cressi, Mares, OSTC and Atomic. Those cable downloads haven't been tried on real hardware yet, so if you own one, please test it. Dives from any computer can also come in through a logbook file.

I use MacDive. Will my logbook come across?

Yes. Export from MacDive as XML or UDDF and import the file. You choose which dives to add, tanks come in as real cylinders (size, pressure, doubles and +), and MacDive's dive IDs are kept, so an export back to MacDive doesn't make duplicates.

Is the Mac app the same app?

It's a separate app with its own beta link. Both apps use the same iCloud logbook, so a dive downloaded on the Mac shows up on your phone. Keep both on the latest build.

What do I need?

An iPhone or iPad with iOS or iPadOS 18 or later, or a Mac with macOS 15 or later. The Apple Watch app needs watchOS 11. To sync between devices, sign in to the same iCloud account on each one.

Could a beta build lose my dives?

It's built not to. Imports never change the file you import from, and restore points can take your logbook back to before an import or merge. Keep your original exports, and if dives go missing or doubled, send a screenshot of Profile → Sync before changing anything.

Can I use the site positions for navigation?

No. Site positions are for logging, not for navigation.
